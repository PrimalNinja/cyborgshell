Barka da zuwa CyborgShell!

CyborgShell na kawo jigon tsofaffin kwamfutoci na gida zuwa na'urorin zamani, yana ba ku damar ƙirƙira, loda lambobi, da haɗin gwiwa a ko'ina, a kowane lokaci. Tare da CyborgShell, zaku iya:

- Ƙirƙirar asusu da shiga a kan na'urori da yawa
- Loda lambobi a cikin JavaScript, kai ko a cikin ƙungiyoyi
- Adana shirye-shiryenku a cikin "sararin" da aka keɓance

An yi wahayi daga sauƙin kwamfutocin gida na 8-bit, CyborgShell yana nufin sake ƙirƙirar gwaninta na nan take, ba tare da wahala ba na kwamfuta ta gargajiya. Yi rajista, shiga, kuma fara loda lambobi cikin seconds!

A cikin CyborgShell, "sararin" ku shine wurin ajiyar ku na kashin kai, wanda kawai ku ke iya samun dama. Hakanan kuna iya bincika wurin jama'a, inda zaku iya ƙirƙira, raba, da gwada tare da wasu.

Raba abu shine muhimmin fasali a cikin CyborgShell. Kamar yadda aka canza diski na floppy a cikin shekarun 80, zaku iya raba sararin ku tare da wasu, da akasin haka. Wannan tsarin raba mai sauƙi yana ba da damar yanayi daban-daban:

- Koyarwa: Raba sarari tare da ɗalibai don koyon jagoranci
- Aikin ƙungiya: Haɗin gwiwa akan ayyuka a cikin sararin ƙungiya na raba
- Abota: Raba sarari juna tare da abokai masu amana

CyborgShell [github.com/PrimalNinja/cyborgshell] an saki shi a ƙarƙashin lasisin software na MIT, saboda "raba yana nufin kula."
