API Mai Bayani

A halin yanzu, yana da wadannan:

(mai dangantaka da gyara)

api.debugObject(<abu>, [<alamar maimaitawa>]);

	nuna abin da aka bayar, idan an gaji da alamar maimaitawa, yana koma zuwa gaskiya ta tsohuwa.

(mai dangantaka da allon)

api.cls();

	share allon

api.print(<fitarwa>);

	buga wasu fitarwa
