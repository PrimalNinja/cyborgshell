Umurnin Fayiloli  
Wannan umarni yana da alaka da fayiloli a cikin sararin fayil ɗinka na yanzu.

beautify <sunan fayil>

	Yana kyautata fayil na JSON ba tare da lalata shirin da aka loda a halin yanzu ba.
	
dir [<sunan fayil>] (ko cat, ls)

	Yana lissafa shirye-shiryen da aka adana. Sunan fayil yana iya ƙunsar alamomin gungun * ko ?.
	
copy <sunan fayil a> <sunan fayil b> (ko cp)

	Yana kwafa fayil daga sunan fayil a zuwa sunan fayil b.
	
del <sunan fayil> (ko delete, era)

	Yana goge shirin.
	
load <sunan fayil>

	Yana loda shirin.
	
ren <sunan fayil a> <sunan fayil b> (ko rename, mv)

	Yana canza sunan fayil daga sunan fayil a zuwa sunan fayil b.
	
run <sunan fayil>

	Yana gudanar da shirin, yana maye gurbin shirin da aka loda a halin yanzu.
	
save <sunan fayil>

	Yana adana shirin.
	
space [<mabuɗin sarari> | <laƙabi>] (ko cs, cd, chdir)

	Yana nuna sararin da kake ciki ko yana canza zuwa sabon.
	
spaces

	Yana lissafa dukkan sararin fayil da kake da izinin shiga.
	
type <sunan fayil> [[daga layi]-[zuwa layi]]

	Yana rubuta shirin ba tare da lalata shirin da aka loda a halin yanzu ba.
